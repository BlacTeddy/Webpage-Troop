About Scouting of America

The Scouting of America (BSA) is one of the nation’s largest and most respected youth development organizations. For over a century, Scouting has helped young people build character, develop leadership skills, learn responsibility, and grow through hands‑on outdoor adventure. Through a program rooted in service, teamwork, and personal growth, Scouts learn to make ethical choices, become confident leaders, and contribute positively to their communities.

Scouting’s mission is simple: to prepare young people to make moral and ethical decisions throughout their lives. Whether through camping, community service, skill‑building, or leadership opportunities, BSA empowers youth to challenge themselves, discover their strengths, and live the values of the Scout Oath and Scout Law.

About Troop 309

Troop 309 is a vibrant, Scout‑led troop based in Brooklyn, NY, committed to helping young people grow into capable, confident leaders. We combine the traditions of Scouting with the energy and diversity of our community, offering a program that is active, inclusive, and focused on real‑world skills.

  • Leadership development – Scouts take the lead in planning meetings, outings, and service projects
  • Outdoor adventure – monthly campouts, hikes, and hands‑on skills
  • Community service – giving back to Brooklyn through meaningful projects
  • Personal growth – building confidence, responsibility, and teamwork
  • Family involvement – welcoming parents and guardians as partners in the Scouting journey
